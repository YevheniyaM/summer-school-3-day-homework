\# Code of Conduct



\## Our Pledge



We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,

respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.



\## Expected Behavior



Participants are expected to:



\- Be respectful and constructive.

\- Give and receive feedback professionally.

\- Focus on the project and its goals.

\- Respect different opinions and backgrounds.



\## Unacceptable Behavior



Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks,

discrimination, threats, and deliberately disruptive behavior.



\## Reporting



If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it

to the project maintainers.



\## Enforcement



Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to behavior

that violates this Code of Conduct.
